Our Story

Built by a dancer who lived the problemfirsthand.

Sophie Gandhorkia — Founder, VYBE

I'm a professional dancer. I've performed on cruise ships, and worked hundreds of live gigs on land — weddings, christenings, corporate events, shop openings and everything in between. VYBE exists to bring high-quality entertainment together in one place that's actually easy to find and hire.

Why I started VYBE

Every performer I know has the same story: incredible talent, no easy way for the people who need them to find them. I spent years dancing at sea and performing at events across the UK, and time and again I saw planners and couples struggling to find entertainers they could trust — scrolling through outdated directories, messaging strangers on social media, hoping for the best.

On the other side, I watched brilliant performers — dancers, singers, musicians, hosts, circus and specialty acts — pour their energy into their craft with no simple way to get discovered by the people actually planning weddings, parties and corporate events.

VYBE is built to close that gap: one trusted platform where exceptional entertainment is easy to find, easy to compare, and easy to book — for everyone from a wedding planner sourcing their tenth event this year, to a couple booking their first.
